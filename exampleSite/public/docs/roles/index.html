Roles and Responsibilities

Responsibilities for maintainers, reviewers, and record-keepers.

December 15, 2025

Maintainers

Maintain structural integrity of governance records and ensure published outputs are current.

Reviewers

Provide feedback, request clarifications, and document unresolved risks.

Record-Keepers

Publish decision records and update indexes.
